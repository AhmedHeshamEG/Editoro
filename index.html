Look

Two effects, both measured from this footage rather than dialled in. The analysis runs once and writes a depth matte and a grade for the project; after that the sliders are instant, and the preview shows exactly what the export will contain.

Breathing is a very slow scale drift under the whole video, so no shot is ever completely still. Named strengths rather than a slider because the tempo is shared: the footage and every graphic on screen move on one clock, and a strength changes how deep the video breathes, never how fast. A punch-in or a ken-burns switches it off for its own span, so two moves never stack.

Thumbnail

A cover for this video, designed once and laid out for both shapes. Pick a frame or drop a picture, choose an arrangement, type the words. The arrangement is a starting point, not a cage - drag anything, scale it, add more.

"First frame" holds the design on the front of the exported video for a single frame, which is what a short opens on. "Image only" leaves the video untouched and just writes the PNG out.
