# Template Pack Context — Ambient

## Identity
- **id:** `ambient`
- **display name:** Ambient
- **category color:** `#4A5568`
- **directive verb:** none

## What it is
A quiet, full-frame charcoal paper collage with subtle deterministic drift. It provides waiting-animation energy behind other ideas without competing with the speaker.

## Fields
No per-instance fields.

## Placement defaults
Both orientations use fullscreen position `0.50, 0.50`, scale `1.00`.

## Animation
- **entrance:** 800 ms fade
- **hold:** slow seeded paper/light drift
- **exit:** 800 ms fade
- **default duration:** 15.0 s

## SFX
None. Ambient is visually quiet and never introduces music.

## Assets
- `ambient-paper.png` — original generated dark paper-collage background

## Rules
- Keep opacity low enough for the speaker to dominate.
- No corkboard, pins, red string, or detective-board imagery.
- Deterministic motion only.

## Out of scope
- Music, particle storms, or bright attention-grabbing loops.
