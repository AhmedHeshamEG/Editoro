# Organic template foley

All included template sounds are real recordings released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). They are trimmed, faded, converted to 48 kHz mono PCM, and normalized conservatively so speech remains dominant.

| Template | Included file | Recording | Processing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highlight | `highlight/marker-sweep.wav` | [Marker pen writing on paper by SSkiba88](https://freesound.org/people/SSkiba88/sounds/751055/) | Single clean marker stroke, 620 ms |
| Keyword | `keyword/paper-pop.wav` | [Short notebook-page sound by sokworks](https://freesound.org/people/sokworks/sounds/860359/) | Complete 194 ms page movement |
| Image pop | `image-pop/paper-slap.wav` | [Paper flick by BananaBread_V2](https://freesound.org/people/BananaBread_V2/sounds/810016/) | One light paper flick, 340 ms |
| Video clip | `video-clip/paper-slap.wav` | [Thin notebook dropped on a desk by an_na_ba_na_na](https://freesound.org/people/an_na_ba_na_na/sounds/486377/) | Impact and natural settling tail, 1.5 s |
| Punch-in | `punch-in/whoosh.wav` | [Bamboo-stick swipe by qubodup](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/60007/) | Complete organic air swipe, 448 ms |
| Screen | `screen/settle.wav` | [Paper rustle by BenjaminNelan](https://freesound.org/people/BenjaminNelan/sounds/353125/) | Soft paper movement, 1.13 s |

Each template applies an additional speech-safe gain in its `template.json`. Highlight repeats the marker recording once per drawn stroke and spaces those events across the sweep animation.
